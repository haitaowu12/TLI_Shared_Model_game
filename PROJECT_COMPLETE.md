## 🎮 Game Features Summary

### Difficulty Levels

1. **Easy – Training** (150s): For learning
2. **Standard – Standard** (120s): Balanced challenge
3. **Difficult – Hardcore** (105s): Skilled players
4. **Expert – Chaos** (90s) ⚡: Ultimate challenge

### Stakeholder Personas

1. **Morgan (D)**: Operations Lead - Action-oriented
2. **Ravi (i)**: Public Trust - Narrative-focused
3. **Aisha (S)**: Safety & Training - Consensus-building
4. **Elena (C)**: Finance & Compliance - Data-driven

### Response Modes

- **Mode A**: Tactical patch (quick, lower score)
- **Mode B**: Strategic pause (balanced)
- **Mode C**: Model reframe (comprehensive, highest score)

### Meter System

- **Shared Model Stability**: Overall coherence
- **Vision Integrity**: Alignment with purpose
- **Stakeholder Confidence**: Trust and buy-in
- **System Health**: Technical wellness
- **Burn Rate**: Resource consumption
